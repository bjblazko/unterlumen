# UX review of every place

*Last modified: 2026-10-21*

A walk through every place, desktop (1440 × 900) and phone (390 × 844), on
the throwaway stage of `e2e/screenshots/stage.mjs`, judged against the ten
principles of `huepattl-rams-design` and the project's own rules
(CLAUDE.md, ADR-0030). These are ideas, not plans; a finding taken up gets
its own feature document.

Primary job of the app: look through photos, keep the good ones, and find
and share them again.

| # | Principle | Rating | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Innovative | Strong | Two installations on one library, local faces, shared filter — real new possibilities, not novelty. |
| 2 | Useful | Adequate | Core tasks are short; some places carry tools for rare jobs in plain view (six maintenance buttons, Clear cache in a view menu). |
| 3 | Aesthetic | Adequate | Tokens hold; but five page-header patterns, three date formats and two folder tiles break the one-system rule. |
| 4 | Understandable | Weak | "Root" as a library's title, up to four counts of one thing, "Delete" that marks, "Done" on places and panels. |
| 5 | Unobtrusive | Adequate | Photos lead; the library detail stacks three toolbars and the filter makes four columns. |
| 6 | Honest | Adequate | Activity is honest; the viewer's Delete does not delete; "Built, not uploaded" wears the time colour. |
| 7 | Long-lasting | Strong | No trend effects; flat, quiet, conventional controls. |
| 8 | Thorough | Weak | Nanosecond timestamps, `0/1` as an aperture, names truncated with room to spare, help text in three sizes. |
| 9 | Environmentally friendly | Strong | No polling for show, no animation at rest, local everything. |
| 10 | As little design as possible | Adequate | Several ways to do one thing: two filter UIs, two close buttons on the filter, About's list twice. |

## Findings

### Structure

1. **Five page-header patterns.** Folders has no header (breadcrumb row, title in the content); Libraries and a library a `bg-2` toolbar; Map, Timeline, Statistics a title with count, filters and lede; People, Settings, About a title over a hairline; Marked for deletion a title row with its actions.
2. **A library stacks three toolbars and calls itself Root.** Library bar (Libraries, Filter, name, Open in Folders, Statistics, Edit library…), the pane's breadcrumb row (home, up, Root, Slideshow, ⋯), the controls row. The title of a library's top folder and of the filter's results is "Root".
3. **One count, up to four times, in three words.** Filter: "8 photos match", "Filter results · 8 photos", "Root 8 photos · 91.7 MB", "8 images". Folders: "50 photos" in the title, "50 images · 3 selected" in the controls, "3 selected" in the selection bar. Marked: "files".
4. **"Done" where nothing is done.** Info panel, Map's photo panel, Statistics' photo panel, Filter (plus a × on its results), Settings (a place). Each closes something different.
5. **The filter is a fourth column.** Sidebar, filter, results and info panel side by side at 1440 px leave the photos about a third of the width.
6. **Two filter interfaces for one filter.** Map, Timeline and Statistics show it as header dropdowns and a Taken slider; a library as the Filter panel with chips (ADR-0050 shares the filter itself).

### Same thing, different look

7. **Folder tiles.** Folders: a plain folder icon; a library: four photos, count and years.
8. **Dates.** ISO in data (`2017-05-06`), `21/10/2026` for Indexed and galleries, `dd.mm.yyyy` in the filter's date fields, `2026-10-21 23:22:51.389677886 +02:00` in a folder's info, `+02:00` on every row of the list view.
9. **Galleries and Destinations.** A list and a table for related things; three words for a gallery not online ("Not online yet", "Not exported yet", "Built, not uploaded"), the last in `--time`, which means time passing; group headers repeat the name ("Website · Website").
10. **The viewer's Delete marks for deletion**, everywhere else called Mark for deletion; it sits in one segment with Crop.

### Too much at once

11. **Edit library: six maintenance buttons** (Scan for new photos, Rebuild metadata & previews, Generate missing previews, Rebuild all previews, Analyse photos again, Remove deleted photos) that overlap.
12. **Viewer toolbar: thirteen controls in one row** — back, name, film strip switch, n of m, full screen, download, zoom out, fit, zoom in, rotate, crop, delete, ⋯.
13. **Folders' ⋯ menu holds Clear cache**, which Settings › Storage also has; the view switches (names, details) are in the menu, hiding the interface is in Settings.
14. **Export mixes three kinds of choice** (a segment for format, radio lists for scale, metadata and output), has an unnamed `~ / •` switch, and strips all EXIF by default.
15. **Rename's tokens are in eight colours**, colour used as decoration; its default pattern puts the date in front of names that already start with it.
16. **Organize: Add target… and New target… side by side**, and a line of keyboard hints in the header.

### Navigation

17. **About is listed twice** (four sidebar entries and the same four on its page); opening it moves Settings up. A library and its parent entry are both filled as current.
18. **Timeline's two bands look the same** (the overview and Shown).
19. **On a phone, Settings, Galleries and Marked for deletion cannot be reached**; Libraries' info is an unlabelled "i".

### Defects found on the way (no choice needed)

- Exposure shows `0/1` for an unknown aperture (Timeline's info panel).
- A folder's Modified shows nanoseconds and a zone.
- The list view truncates file names with room to spare, and loses "In library …" in the breadcrumb.
- Help text comes in three sizes (`text-xs` under Storage rows, `text-sm` ledes, `text-base` under switches and in dialogs).

## Not checked

People with faces (the stage has no face model), the 3D views (no WebGPU headless), dark theme beyond Folders, keyboard-only use, long names and huge numbers, Organize on a phone, every dialog of Galleries and Destinations.
