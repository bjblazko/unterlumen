# Things: animals, objects and scenes in the photos

*Last modified: 2026-10-24*

## Summary

Find animals, objects and scenes — a bird, a car, a house, trees, nature,
the sea — in library photos, on this computer. Search and filter by them,
search by any words, count them in the statistics, and keep them in the
sidecars as keywords. Telling individual pets apart comes later. See
[ADR-0054](../../architecture/adr/0054-things-found-on-this-computer.md).

## Details

- Statistics → Things offers "Download and turn on" (447 MB: CLIP and YOLOX,
  ONNX Runtime unless People downloaded it).
- A pass after every scan finds the things; the sidecar gets the keywords
  and Unterlumen's record of them first, then the index.
- A search bar above every place suggests people and things and searches by
  any words; a choice opens Libraries with the filter set.
- The shared filter (Map, Timeline, Statistics) and the Libraries filter
  know Thing; the Libraries filter knows Shows (words).
- A photo's info panel lists its things; × takes one away for good.
- Statistics → Things: met most, over time, together.
