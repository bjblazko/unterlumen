# Map: photos where they were taken

*Last modified: 2026-09-27*

## Summary

A new place, Map, shows every photo with a location from every library on one large map. Photos taken close together form a group, drawn as a round thumbnail of the newest photo with the number of photos; zooming in splits the groups until each photo stands at its own place. Requested by the owner on 2026-09-27; the design decisions are in [ADR-0039](../../architecture/adr/0039-map-place.md).

## Details

- **Where.** A sidebar group "Explore" with one entry, Map (`#map`, key 7), and a tab on a phone.
- **What.** Every indexed photo with usable coordinates, from all libraries (`GET /api/library/geo`). Photos outside a library do not appear.
- **Groups.** MapLibre clusters the points in the browser. Each marker shows the newest photo of its group, round, and the count when there is more than one. A marker is a button with a label ("12 photos, the newest from 3 May 2024").
- **Click.** A group zooms in until it splits. A group whose photos share one spot opens in the viewer, newest first; a single photo opens in the viewer. From the map the viewer is read-only: no crop, no marking for deletion.
- **Photos in view.** A "Photos" button in the head, with the number of photos in the part of the map on screen, opens a column beside the map (the lower half on a phone): those photos as square tiles, newest first. It follows the map as it moves and zooms, so zooming into a group shows that group. Done or Escape closes it; it is closed at first, and the browser remembers whether it was open. A tile opens the viewer on the photos of the column. Asked for by the owner after trying the map: "a filtered grid of the photos in the count bubble", with the full map staying the default.
- **Time.** A two-handle slider over the months from the oldest to the newest photo narrows the map; the head says "8 of 25 photos". Undated photos are shown only while the whole span is chosen. The slider moves with a pointer, a finger or the keyboard; the handle being moved turns orange. The library filter's sliders are the same component now.
- **Map.** Grey tiles from OpenFreeMap, `positron` in the light theme and `fiord` (a lighter blue-grey; `dark` was too dark) in the dark one, switched with the theme.
- **Style.** A switch in the head, "Style: Colour / Grey", shows the map in OpenFreeMap's colour style (`liberty`, light in both themes). Grey is the default; the choice is remembered per browser.
- **States.** While loading: "Reading the locations…" (after 400 ms). No library: a sentence with a link to Libraries. Libraries without located photos: "None of the N photos in your libraries has a location." Nothing in the chosen period: a sentence on the map. No connection to OpenFreeMap or no MapLibre: a sentence saying so.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Map is a place in the sidebar under Explore, reachable at `#map` and with key 7, and a tab on a phone.
- [x] Only photos with a location appear, from every library; 0,0 and out-of-range coordinates are left out.
- [x] Each marker shows the newest photo of its group, round, with the count when it holds more than one.
- [x] Zooming in splits the groups; clicking a group zooms in until it splits.
- [x] A group at one spot and a single photo open in the viewer, which is read-only there; Escape returns to the map.
- [x] The photos column is closed at first, shows the photos in view newest first, follows the map, opens a photo in the viewer, closes with Done or Escape, and is remembered.
- [x] The time slider narrows the photos on the map, and works with pointer and keyboard.
- [x] The map is grey and follows the theme; the Style switch shows it in colour and is remembered.
- [x] Empty, loading and failure states are sentences.
- [x] Go tests for the query and the endpoint; `map.spec.js` covers the place.
