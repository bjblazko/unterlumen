# Timeline: every library photo on one time axis

*Last modified: 2026-09-27*

## Summary

A new place **Timeline** in the sidebar group Explore, below Map. It shows every
photo of every library by the date it was taken, regardless of folder or
library. On a desk the photos run from left (oldest) to right (newest) in a
band of several rows. Below the band is a time axis: a graph of how many photos
were taken when (no numbers), year and month labels, and a frame marking what
the band shows right now. Moving the frame scrolls the band, and scrolling the
band moves the frame. An overview above the axis limits the range, for example
to 2020–2025. On a phone the photos run top to bottom, newest first, with a
vertical scrubber down the right edge.

The owner chose design B (desktop) and Mobile 1 (phone) from the Timeline
clickdummy (https://claude.ai/artifact/N2RjBab31exPDAy4A2Xxh5). The decisions
and their reasons are in
[ADR-0040](../../architecture/adr/0040-timeline-place.md).

## Details

### What is shown

- Every photo with an EXIF date taken, from every library, sorted by that date.
- A photo that sits in more than one library (the same SHA-256) appears once.
- Photos without a date are left out. The head says how many: "212 without a
  date are not shown".
- Photos marked missing are left out, as on the Map.

### Desktop (wider than 700 px)

- **Head**: "Timeline" and, in mono, the count and the span ("31,204 photos ·
  Jan 2009 – Sep 2026"). The sentence about photos without a date follows. The
  ⋯ menu (`Menu`) holds **Rows: 2 / 3 / 4** (default 3, remembered per
  browser).
- **Band**: justified tiles of equal height in 2–4 rows. Each photo goes into
  the row that ends furthest left, so time runs left to right. A month starts a
  new column with a month label ("Aug 2019") in mono above it. The mouse wheel
  scrolls sideways.
- **Time bar** (surface step `--bg-2`, no hairline):
  1. **Overview**: the whole span as a small graph with year labels. Two
     bracket handles (the `RangeSlider` look) limit the range, snapped to
     months. Dragging the space between them moves the range. Next to it:
     the range in mono and a "Show all" button.
  2. **Axis**: the limited range stretched across the width, as a graph
     (`--chart-seq-*`, step shape, photos per day within each bin). It carries
     year lines and year labels, and month names where there is room. The
     **frame** marks the days the band shows. Drag it, or click the axis to
     jump there.
- **Frame and band are coupled**: dragging the frame scrolls the band and
  scrolling the band moves the frame. While the frame is being dragged it is
  `--accent`, the current value of something adjusted right now. At rest it is
  `--fg`.
- **Photos**: a click selects a photo; while the info panel is open (I, or
  its button) it shows the selected photo. It does not open by itself:
  opening narrows the band and would move the tiles away from under a double
  click. A double-click or Enter opens the viewer, read-only as from the Map.
  The left and right arrow keys move the selection.

### Phone (700 px or narrower)

- **Head**: "Timeline" and the count.
- **List**: justified rows, newest at the top, grouped by month with a
  heading that stays at the top while its month scrolls by.
- **Scrubber**: a 60 px column down the right edge, with the newest date at
  the top. It carries year marks and one bar per month for the number of
  photos, without numbers. Put a finger anywhere on it and drag; a bubble
  shows the month. The list follows the finger, and the thumb follows the
  list.
- A tap opens the viewer.
- There is no range limit on the phone: the scrubber is the overview.

### Loading and memory

- **Skeleton**, loaded once when the place opens: for each photo only its day
  and aspect ratio, as compact arrays. The graph, the axis and the layout are
  all built from it. The browser keeps it in typed arrays: 6 bytes per photo.
- **Details**, loaded by index range for what is in view plus a margin: photo
  ID, library ID, filename and date as ISO text. Pages of up to 500 photos are
  kept in a cache with a fixed size; the oldest page is dropped first.
- **Tiles**: only tiles in view plus a margin exist in the DOM. A tile that
  leaves releases its image. The number of `img` elements stays bounded
  however long the stream is.
- **Version**: the skeleton carries a version. When a library changes, the
  details request answers `409`, and the place reloads the skeleton and stays
  at the same date.
- Loading goes through `Activity`: nothing for 400 ms, then words.
