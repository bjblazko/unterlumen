# Videos: listed, filtered, played

*Last modified: 2026-10-30*

## Summary

Immich is gone from the home server, so Unterlumen is the owner's main tool
and has to show videos. The owner asked for videos to be listed and
filterable (shown or hidden), watchable, with a small moving preview, and not
editable or renamable, using the tools Unterlumen already has. Recorded in
[ADR-0058](../../architecture/adr/0058-videos-listed-and-played-never-edited.md).

## Details

- **Listed** in Folders and libraries: MP4, M4V, MOV, WebM, MKV, AVI, MTS,
  M2TS, 3GP. Their still frame comes from ffmpeg.
- **The tile** shows the length (`0:42`) bottom right. While the pointer
  rests on it, it plays a 3 s, 320 px, silent clip, made by ffmpeg on the
  first hover and cached. Phones show the still frame.
- **The viewer** plays MP4, M4V, MOV and WebM with the browser's controls;
  Space plays or pauses. Others say they cannot be played and offer the file.
  There is no zoom or crop, and the ⋯ offers no editing.
- **Live Photos**: a `.mov` beside a HEIC/JPEG of the same name is not listed.
  The photo carries "Live", and the viewer's Live plays the clip once.
- **Videos** switch in the ⋯ of Folders and of a library, remembered per
  browser. Hidden, videos also leave the library filter's results.
- **Kind** chip in the library filter: photos or videos. The shared filter of
  Map, Statistics and the Timeline has Kind too.
- **Counts**: a library's card and a folder's head count photos and videos
  apart.
- **Metadata**: length, date, place, camera and size from `ffmpeg -i`, so
  videos appear in the Timeline and on the Map.
- **Not for videos**: crop, set location, rename, export, galleries, faces,
  things and the slideshow.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] A folder with videos lists them with their length; the place's head counts photos and videos separately
- [x] The clip of a Live Photo is not listed; its photo says "Live" and plays it in the viewer
- [x] Hovering a video tile plays a short silent clip; the clip is made once and cached
- [x] The viewer plays a browser-playable video and offers the file for one it cannot play
- [x] Videos can be hidden and shown again from the ⋯, in Folders and in a library
- [x] The library filter finds photos only or videos only (Kind)
- [x] A library indexes videos with date, place, camera and length; Faces and Things leave them out
- [x] Crop, location, rename and export are not offered for videos
- [x] The shared filter of Map, Statistics and the Timeline narrows to photos or videos
- [x] A library's card counts photos and videos apart; a video with a place carries the GPS badge
- [x] e2e spec `e2e/specs/videos.spec.js`
