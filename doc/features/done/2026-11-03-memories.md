# Memories: on this day, trips, people, rediscovered

*Last modified: 2026-11-03*

## Summary

A place, Memories (Erinnerungen), that brings old photos back the way
Immich, Apple Photos and Google Photos do. At the top it shows the photos
of today's date in earlier years. Below that, cards lead to a trip, a
person, this month in earlier years and rediscovered photos. The photos
are chosen without star ratings, by what they show: people first, then
variety, then the unusual. See
[ADR-0064](../../architecture/adr/0064-memories-without-ratings.md).

## Details

- The place sits in Explore, before Map, and is the sixth tab on the phone.
- **On this day {n} years ago** shows one row per earlier year with photos
  on today's month-day. On 28 February of a non-leap year, photos from
  29 February count too.
- **More memories** shows at most one card per kind:
  - **Trip:** a trip that began within ±7 days of today in an earlier
    year, otherwise one chosen by daily rotation. A trip is days more
    than 100 km from home, with at most 2 days between them (the rule
    Statistics uses), named by its country.
  - **Person:** a birthday in the next 7 days ("Anna turns 8 on …",
    one pick per year of life). Otherwise a person with photos in at
    least 3 years ("Anna over the years").
  - **Month:** this month in earlier years, one pick per year.
  - **Rediscovered:** five photos a day from the rarest 5 %, rotated so
    that nothing repeats for a long time.
- **Selection:**
  1. Leave out videos, screenshots, documents and photos found to be
     only blurred, underexposed or overexposed.
  2. Split the photos into moments wherever more than 30 minutes pass,
     and after two hours at the latest.
     Take one pick per moment, and none that looks like an earlier pick
     (dHash ≤ 10).
  3. Prefer photos with named people, then photos that are rare in the
     library (things, main hue, low or high key, far from home).
  4. Take three to seven picks per memory, as many as the day chooses; a
     person or a month takes some of its years, not every one. The
     choice stays the same through the day.
- **One sheet:** every memory is a block, a collage without gaps whose
  photos take posters, panoramas, squares or their own shape by chance.
  Blocks stand side by side in bands of one height; the order changes on
  every visit, and Shuffle lays it out anew. No photo appears twice.
- A block's caption opens a page with its own address
  (`#memories/<kind>/<key>`): the picks as one collage, then all photos
  of the memory. A tile opens the viewer.
- Memories follow the shared filter (ADR-0050) except its months. Stars
  never count.
- Nothing new is stored: no hiding, no record of what was opened.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Memories is a place in the sidebar (Explore) and the phone's tab bar, with the address `#memories`
- [x] On this day shows a block for each earlier year, with three to seven picks and a count
- [x] One sheet: blocks of collages in bands, formats by chance, order shuffled per visit, Shuffle, no photo twice
- [x] Leap day: on 28 February of a non-leap year, photos from 29 February are included
- [x] Bursts give one pick per moment; screenshots, documents and failed photos never appear
- [x] Photos with named people come before others; then rare photos
- [x] Trip, person, month and rediscovered cards appear when they have photos, and are absent when they have none
- [x] A birthday in the next seven days gives the person card with the age
- [x] Each row and card leads to a page with its picks and all of its photos; counts match
- [x] A tile opens the read-only viewer
- [x] The shared filter narrows the memories; star ratings are never used
- [x] The same day gives the same page; the next day may differ
- [x] Six tabs fit a 360 px wide phone without horizontal scrolling
- [x] Go tests, a plan test, an e2e spec and a probe on a copy of a real library
